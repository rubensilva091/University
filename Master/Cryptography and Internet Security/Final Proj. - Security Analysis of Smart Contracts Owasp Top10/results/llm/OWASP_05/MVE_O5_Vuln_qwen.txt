### Vulnerability 1: **Replay Attack**  
1. **Vulnerability Type**: Replay Attack  
2. **Severity**: Critical  
3. **Affected Function**: `claim(...)` (lines 13–25)  
4. **Description**:  
   - The `deadline` parameter is unused and not included in the `digest` calculation.  
   - No nonce or signature consumption mechanism is implemented, allowing the same signature to be reused indefinitely to claim funds.  
   - This enables attackers to replay the same transaction multiple times, inflating balances arbitrarily.  
5. **Recommended Fix**:  
   - Include a unique nonce per signature in the `digest` and track consumed signatures in a mapping.  
   - Enforce `deadline` by checking `block.timestamp <= deadline` and include it in the `digest`.
