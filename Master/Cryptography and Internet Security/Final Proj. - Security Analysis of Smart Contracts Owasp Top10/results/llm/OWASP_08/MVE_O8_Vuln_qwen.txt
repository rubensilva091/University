1. **Vulnerability Type**: Reentrancy  
   **Severity**: Critical  
   **Affected Function**: `withdraw` (lines 11-16)  
   **Description**: The contract performs an external call (`msg.sender.call{value: amount}("")`) **before** updating the user's balance. This allows an attacker with a malicious contract to recursively call `withdraw` during the external call, draining funds before the state is updated.  
   **Fix**: Update the user's balance **before** making the external call to prevent reentrancy. Replace the order of operations:  
   ```solidity
   balanceOf[msg.sender] -= amount;
   (bool ok, ) = msg.sender.call{value: amount}("");
   require(ok, "transfer failed");
   ```
