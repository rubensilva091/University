### Security Vulnerability Report

**1. Vulnerability Type:** Denial of Service (DoS) / Stuck Funds
*   **Severity:** Medium
*   **Affected Function and Line Number(s):**
    *   `setTreasury` (L30)
    *   `emergencyWithdraw` (L35)
*   **Brief Description of the Risk:**
    The `setTreasury` function allows the contract owner to designate any `address payable` as the `treasury`. If the owner mistakenly sets this address to a contract that cannot receive Ether (e.g., a contract whose `receive()` or `fallback()` function consistently reverts) or even to the contract's own address (`address(this)`), then the `emergencyWithdraw` function will always fail. This would effectively lock all deposited Ether within the contract, making them permanently unrecoverable.
*   **Recommended Fix:**
    Implement an additional function, callable only by the owner, that allows specifying an alternative recipient for withdrawals in an emergency, or add a check in `setTreasury` to prevent setting the `treasury` to `address(this)`.

**2. Vulnerability Type:** Centralization Risk
*   **Severity:** Informational
*   **Affected Function and Line Number(s):**
    *   `owner` (L5)
    *   `constructor` (L18-L19)
    *   `onlyOwner` modifier (L12-L15)
*   **Brief Description of the Risk:**
    The contract design is highly centralized, with a single `owner` address having exclusive control over critical functions such as setting the treasury and initiating emergency withdrawals of all funds. While this may be the intended design, it introduces a single point of failure and places significant trust in the owner. If the owner's key is compromised or the owner acts maliciously, the funds could be at risk.
*   **Recommended Fix:**
    For increased decentralization or robustness against single points of failure, consider implementing a multi-signature wallet for the `owner` role or explore decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) governance mechanisms. (Note: This is often a design choice rather than a strict vulnerability, depending on the project's goals).
